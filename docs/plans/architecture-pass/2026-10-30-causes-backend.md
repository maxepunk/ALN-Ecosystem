### B-c1. One fact, two builders

**Design cause.** The snapshot and the live update of one fact are built by separate pieces of code, and each sender of the snapshot builds its own argument list by hand, so the two drift in keys, filters and timing.

**Findings.** B-30, B-31, B-68, B-85, B-95.
