## Suggestions (what the picture leaves out that a reader needs)

1. **The design studio, release and proofs have no arrows.** They float in the top band. Draw the studio's link: an arrow from Design studio to Answering labelled "publish, apply, rehearse, by message" (4.3's config tool arrow; 4.1 P15 "never by file or import"). One arrow from Joins and proofs to Engine instance, "its own engine, never the live one", and one from Release to Verdict, "what is installed, checked", would show how they touch the engine (4.2: "only through its doors and its names").
2. **How the pack reaches every device.** The Pack channel has no arrow to any device. An arrow from Pack channel up to the top band, "the pack, fetched and stamped", shows the first promise's travel half (4.1 P2; 4.3).
3. **Room commands that go straight to Equipment.** 4.2 has Answering using Equipment directly (volume, routing, speakers). Either label the existing arrow "cues, videos, holds" or add a second branch to Equipment, "volume, routing, speakers".
4. **Who else quotes the Verdict.** The Show runner quotes it at cue time (edge P9 to P10), the studio shows its badges on a draft, and Release checks with its words. Grouping the Verdict under "The life of a game" with one link to the Lifecycle hides that it is the one verdict for the editor, the preflight, the start gate and a cue-time refusal (4.1 P10).
5. **Equipment's bindings and every part's names.** Equipment reads which real instrument each name binds to from Venue fit (edge P8 to P12), and Equipment, Connections and the Lifecycle take their names from the Engine instance. One arrow, Venue fit to Equipment "which instrument plays each name", would show why the room can differ by venue.
6. **The starting point that builds every part.** 4.2 names one composition root above every part, which boot, Reset System and each test call with an instance. A one-line note under the foundations band would answer "who builds all this".
7. **Arrows from the screens.** "Requests and replies" leaves the band under GM tablets only; phones and hardware scanners also send scans through Answering (4.3). Start it under the whole Screens box, or label it "taps, scans and commands".
8. **The night's record during play.** The box says "written at the end of a game". The shape has it capturing the roster, the GM's notes and the accusation during play, and the owner and designer reading it afterwards in the studio's Review (4.1 P5; edge P15 to P5). "Gathered during play, closed at the end" is closer.
9. **Joins and proofs' first job.** Its box says only "the tests, on an engine of their own". Its interface starts with every shape two parts share written once and both sides reading it (4.1 P16). "Shared shapes written once; tests on their own engine" fits in two lines.
10. **A key for the marks.** Say in the figure key that the two doors and their arrows are drawn in the accent colour, and that the dashed box (outside programs) is not a part.
11. **Verdict wording.** "Not installed tonight" is one of dormant's two doors; an operator can also put a service out of service (CONTEXT.md section 4, "Dormant vs fault"). "Go, no-go, not in use tonight" covers both, and "what the game needs against what the kit has" would say what the verdict compares.
