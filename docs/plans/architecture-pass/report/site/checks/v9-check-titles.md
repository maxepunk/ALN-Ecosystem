# Version 9 check: the titles of the quality targets and extension cases

Checked 2026-11-01 against site plan v2 (sections 4 and 6: each target and case gains a `title` of three to eight plain words, unique, with no id or number, in the jargon table's wordings) and against each item's own fields. Files: `requirements/2026-11-01-quality-targets.json` (42 targets) and `requirements/2026-11-01-extension-cases.json` (24 cases), with the writer's log `requirements/titles-log.md`. Every item was read in full (situation or case, what it should need, what it must not need, the number to set, status) and its title judged against that text.

Result: 66 items checked, 7 conflicts. No item lacks a title, no title is duplicated, and no other field changed.

## What I checked and found right

- **Presence and form.** All 66 items carry a `title`: 42 targets (QT1 to QT43, with QT28 moved to the build plan as the targets log records at its row 18) and 24 cases (EX1 to EX24). Every title has three to eight words (counted by script). None carries an id, a digit, a sequencing number or an em dash. Number words (two-hour, two-tap, first, second, third) state facts from the item, not ids.
- **Uniqueness.** No two titles match, compared without case, within or across the two files. None repeats a promise, flaw, decision or question title found in `report/site/decisions.json`, `report/2026-10-31-cause-cards.json`, `report/2026-10-31-engine-jobs.json`, `report/2026-11-01-engine-shape.json` or the headings of `DECISIONS.md`. Titles that touch the same ground stay distinct: "A trial copy never touches the live show", "A test run cannot touch the live show", and the copies target.
- **No other field changed.** Field by field, each item matches the committed version (commit 840069a7) except for the new `title`. `git diff` shows 66 inserted lines, all `title`, and no deleted line. The `intro` of each file is unchanged, and so are the item order, ids, statuses, promises, numbers to set and every text.
- **Log agrees with the files.** Each of the 66 lines in `requirements/titles-log.md` matches the title in the JSON exactly.
