output, in `docs/plans/architecture-pass/DECISIONS.md` and in two working
files: `rework/sorting.md` (Table 2, the QT2 row) and
`report/site/checks/jargon-table.md` (row 17). I read the whole record

owner's quoted words with `rework/2026-11-02-owner-comments.md`, and I
checked the QT2 row against `backend/src/utils/processMonitor.js` and
`backend/src/services/vlcMprisService.js`. I edited nothing but this
report. Two notes on method. The record uses straight quotes for titles
throughout (236 straight, no typographic quotes), so I did not count
straight quotes in changed lines as rule breaks; if the typographic rule

outside the hand fixes. Version 10 is built but not yet published
