## Scope and method

I checked the twelve findings of `v10-review-outside.md`, O1 to O12, against the published text (`v10text/decisions.md`, `design.md`, `index.md`, `today.md`, `requirements.md`), the sources (`report/site/v9/decisions.json` and `design.json`, the two requirements files), `DECISIONS.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, the panel's brief, the owner's comments of 2026-11-02, and the code under `/home/user/ALN-Ecosystem`. O11 and O12 each hold several separate points, so they get one row per point.

## Result

Every problem the review names is real, and its code facts hold. The line references are right to within two lines. Its field names in `decisions.json` and `design.json` are right. No finding is refuted whole. Four rows stand as proposed (O3 part 3c, O5, O6 part 2 and O12 part 1). The rest need a different change, and inside them I refuse seven parts:

1. O4's line to the owner on merging two design rounds and moving the protocol after them. The architects can weigh that; the owner cannot yet. It fails the fourth test, and the review asks the panel the same thing in the same finding. It goes to the panel only.
2. O3's reading stated inside an option ("The agent reads this as inside your decision"). A reading of the owner's words that no earlier decision settles goes under "Readings of your words, to confirm".
3. O3's third and fourth asks. Whether a kit carries a spare is a production's setting under the owner's decision of 2026-11-02. The TV's clock is not B's scene: with the show machine dead the TV has no picture.
4. O6's third option in J. Staging what the architects see is how the panel runs, which the owner made the agent's call.
5. O7's sentence that the first release "is tested and supported on ALN's kit" and its choice of what the GM app reads. The record leaves that line to the design rounds and the build plan, and then to the owner. A call cannot draw it now.
6. O9's third ask (may a phones-only game put the whole game on each phone). There is only one answer consistent with the owner's words, so it is a fact to state, not a question.
7. O11's design aim of "a few hundred devices". The setting's "a few dozen devices" is in the owner's decision on what the panel sees, and nothing in the record supports a tenfold figure.

Five things the review missed are at the end, V1 to V5. The first four change what the owner decides or what the design covers.

Severity: I agree with the review's grades except O4, which I rate medium once its owner ask is removed, and O10, which I rate low to medium.
