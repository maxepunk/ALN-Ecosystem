# Jargon check: parts.html

Read 2026-11-01, as the owner would, every sentence of `site/parts.html`: the status line, the three intro paragraphs, the qualities table, the layers caption and "which part uses which" note, the moments key and all five moment diagrams (every arrow label and frame label), the seventeen part cards (first paragraph, Today, Runs on, Where it can be swapped), "Which part would run where" and its diagram (box and arrow labels), the tangles note and all thirteen tangles, the alternatives intro and all eight rows, and the footer. Also checked: the `title` and `aria-label` attributes (only flaw titles and figure names; nothing to change).

Source keys: **SJ** = `report/2026-11-01-engine-shape.json` (field path, then file line); **BS** = `report/build_site.py` (constant or function, line); **FJ** = `site/front.json`. Part cards are named by part id in SJ (`parts[P13]` is Engine instance). A card's "Today" line is not its own field: `build()` (BS:1897-1898) takes it from `mechanisms[i].plain` by `MECH_PART` (BS:203), and `today_of()` (BS:210) keeps the first sentence after the definition that contains "today". A rewrite of a Today sentence must keep "Today " at its start. The quality row "Who decides when there is no engine" takes its "Today:" from `mechanisms[10]` the same way (BS:1490).

Not listed (the owner's words, or defined in the words box): promise, part, quality, moment, design flaw, candidate shape, venue technician, standalone, the director, module, interface, seam, adapter, door, a device's pass, paper or live, not installed tonight, the seventeen part names, the flaw ids and titles, the promise titles, Reset System, preflight, held items, test rig, toy game, verdict badges, watch-only pass, boot.
