Read for it: `../DECISIONS.md` whole; `2026-11-02-owner-comments.md`; `sorting.md`; the two requirements files; `../report/2026-10-31-engine-jobs.json` and `../report/2026-10-31-standalone-lines.json`; the version 9 overlays `front.json`, `design.json`, `rules.json`, `decisions.json` and `changes.json`; `../report/build_site.py` where it renders those fields; `../panel/2026-11-01-panel-brief.md`; the jargon table.

"Should say" is the proposed text, written to the site's rules: plain short sentences, no metaphors, the jargon table's wordings, decisions as `[[dec:...]]` markers where the page links them, no visible ids, no question letters outside the Decisions page. In the site's own prose the owner is "you"; in the shared texts the panel also reads (the requirements' situation, case, numberToSet and why fields, the standing rules, the promise data, the standalone lines) the owner stays "the owner". Where a proposed text rests on a reading of the owner's words that the record marks "to be confirmed", the text says so and does not write the reading as the owner's. The decision record is quoted from `../DECISIONS.md`; the owner's own words are quoted from `2026-11-02-owner-comments.md`.

The Decisions page (`decisions.json`) is being rewritten whole under "The Decisions page holds only your decisions" (its sorting is in `sorting.md`), so its rows are not here; the last section names what the other pages must change in step with it.
